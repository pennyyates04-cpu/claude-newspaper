# Daily Tech, Business & Sales News Guidelines

Audience: a Sales Development Representative (SDR) at Oracle NetSuite working toward
becoming an Account Executive. The goal is to build knowledge of technology, business,
SaaS, finance, and sales — and sharpen conversations with prospects and executives.

Give 5–8 of the most relevant and interesting stories, prioritizing signal over volume:
important + surprising + relevant to business + useful for career growth. 5 excellent
stories beat 20 mediocre ones.

## 1. AI & Technology

AI and generative AI; AI agents and autonomous business workflows; enterprise AI; cloud
computing; cybersecurity; data analytics; automation; SaaS; fintech; ERP, CRM, HCM, and
other enterprise software; AI infrastructure and semiconductors; tech startups; major
acquisitions, partnerships, and product launches. Pay particular attention to how new
technology changes the way businesses operate and spend money.

## 2. Business & Economy

Major companies and earnings; IPOs, acquisitions, mergers, layoffs; interest rates and the
economy; inflation and spending; small/midsize business trends; entrepreneurship and
startups; private equity and VC; supply chains; global business trends; industry growth
and contraction; corporate technology spending. Explain complex developments in a way
someone early in a sales career can follow.

## 3. Oracle & NetSuite (especially important)

Oracle, Oracle Cloud, Oracle NetSuite, NetSuite ERP, NetSuite AI, Oracle's AI strategy,
Oracle database and cloud infrastructure, Oracle acquisitions, NetSuite product launches
and updates, NetSuite competitors, ERP trends, and how companies are modernizing their
financial and business systems. When something happens at Oracle or NetSuite, explain why
it matters to customers and prospects.

## 4. Competitive Intelligence

Track: SAP, Microsoft, Salesforce, Workday, Intuit, QuickBooks, Sage, Acumatica, Infor,
HubSpot, Zoho, ServiceNow, and other important ERP/CRM/accounting/business-software
companies. Don't just report launches — explain how each move changes the competitive
landscape and could affect NetSuite's positioning.

## 5. Sales Intelligence

Modern B2B sales, prospecting, cold calling, sales psychology, buyer behavior, discovery,
objection handling, enterprise sales, SaaS sales, account-based selling, sales technology,
AI in sales, and changes in how executives make purchasing decisions. Prioritize new
research, trends, or strategies over generic sales advice.

## 6. Prospect & Industry Intelligence

Trends affecting the kinds of businesses that could buy NetSuite: SaaS companies,
professional services, manufacturing, wholesale distribution, retail and e-commerce,
healthcare, financial services, media and entertainment, software companies, international
businesses, fast-growing startups and mid-market companies. Where possible, answer: "If I
were prospecting this type of company, why would this news matter to them?"

## 7. Make every story actionable

For each important story, give:

- **What happened:** concise explanation
- **Why it matters:** the business significance
- **Sales angle:** how this could affect a company that might buy NetSuite or other
  enterprise software
- **SDR takeaway:** what to understand or remember
- **Conversation starter:** a natural question or talking point for a prospect — never
  scripted or overly "salesy" (e.g. "I saw that companies in your industry are
  increasingly investing in AI-driven financial automation. Has that been something your
  team has been looking at?")

## 8. Teach business vocabulary

Whenever an important business, financial, technology, or sales term appears, briefly
explain it in plain English — building fluency in the language used by CEOs, CFOs, CIOs,
CTOs, VPs of Finance/Operations, sales leaders, and investors.

## 9. Signal over noise

Rank each story:

- 🔥 **Must Know** — important for career or sales conversations
- 💡 **Worth Knowing** — useful business/technology knowledge
- 👀 **Watch This** — an emerging trend that could become important

## 10. Sources

Prioritize reliable sources and original reporting: company announcements and investor
relations pages, SEC filings, major business and technology publications, earnings calls,
academic/business research, industry reports, and original product announcements. Link to
the original source rather than commentary about it when possible.

## Overall goal

Make the briefing build real understanding: **what happened → why it matters → who is
affected → what it means for businesses → what it means for NetSuite → how to use it as an
SDR.** Over time this should build the business judgment, technology knowledge,
competitive awareness, and executive-level vocabulary needed to become an excellent
Account Executive.
