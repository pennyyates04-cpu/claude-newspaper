Anthropic's Blockbuster IPO Slips Again — Landing Just Before the Midterms

Anthropic confidentially filed a draft S-1 with the SEC on June 1, 2026, and had targeted a public prospectus and listing for late September or early October. Reuters has since reported the timeline slipping roughly another month: marketing is now expected to start in mid-October at the earliest, with the listing landing just days before the November 3 U.S. midterm elections. Anthropic is reportedly targeting a valuation as high as $2 trillion — one of the largest IPOs ever attempted — with Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase, and Citigroup involved, alongside a reported $15 billion credit facility. Separately, Anthropic is asking shareholders to approve a structure giving CEO Dario Amodei and six co-founders 50.1% of voting power post-IPO, modeled on Palantir's setup, though founders wouldn't control board elections. A slipping timeline landing right next to a major election, at a nearly $2 trillion ask, is a real test of how much appetite public markets have left for AI-company valuations after two years of AI-driven stock gains.

It's a sign Beijing may be easing its own restrictions on advanced Nvidia hardware for select companies, even as the underlying U.S.-China chip standoff continues — and it shows Nvidia designing new products explicitly to work around export-control thresholds.

New Study: Cold Calls Still Beat AI-Written Emails 6 to 1

A study released September 1, 2026 by ValueSelling Associates and Selling Power, surveying 153 sales leaders and reps, found that sellers rate human-to-human cold calling six times more effective than AI-assisted personalized email for reaching new prospects — 47% versus 8% — with cold calling ranked as the most effective prospecting method behind only client referrals. Yet 46% of sellers say reps still give up too easily when cold calling, and 39% admit they're afraid to pick up the phone, both down modestly from 53% and 48% in a comparable 2018 study. The same research found 55% of sellers now use AI to draft prospecting emails and research prospects, and 46% use it to personalize outreach. Despite years of AI-outreach tooling, the highest-effort, most human channel is still rated the most effective way to break through — a useful reality check against the assumption that AI-generated volume can substitute for a real conversation.

Sales angle
For an SDR competing for a buyer's attention, this is direct validation that picking up the phone still outperforms an AI-personalized email, even if AI is a useful research and prep tool beforehand.
SDR takeaway
Using AI to prepare for a call (research, personalization, call notes) and using AI to replace the call itself are very different strategies — the data says only the first one still wins.
