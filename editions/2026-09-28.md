## New Study: Cold Calls Still Beat AI-Written Emails 6 to 1

**What happened:** A study released September 1, 2026 by ValueSelling Associates and Selling Power, surveying 153 sales leaders and reps, found that sellers rate human-to-human cold calling six times more effective than AI-assisted personalized email for reaching new prospects — 47% versus 8% — with cold calling ranked as the most effective prospecting method behind only client referrals. Yet 46% of sellers say reps still give up too easily when cold calling, and 39% admit they're afraid to pick up the phone, both down modestly from 53% and 48% in a comparable 2018 study. The same research found 55% of sellers now use AI to draft prospecting emails and research prospects, and 46% use it to personalize outreach.
**Why it matters:** Despite years of AI-outreach tooling, the highest-effort, most human channel is still rated the most effective way to break through — a useful reality check against the assumption that AI-generated volume can substitute for a real conversation.

**Sales angle:** For an SDR competing for a buyer's attention, this is direct validation that picking up the phone still outperforms an AI-personalized email, even if AI is a useful research and prep tool beforehand.
**SDR takeaway:** Using AI to prepare for a call (research, personalization, call notes) and using AI to replace the call itself are very different strategies — the data says only the first one still wins.
