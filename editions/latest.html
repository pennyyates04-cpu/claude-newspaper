Critical SonicWall SMA1000 Flaw (CVSS 10.0) Exploited Within Days of Disclosure

SonicWall patched CVE-2026-102255 on October 6 — a pre-authentication server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability in the WorkPlace interface of its SMA1000 remote-access appliances (the 6210, 7210, and 8200v models), rated a maximum CVSS score of 10.0. Within days, researchers and outlets including The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, and SecurityWeek reported exploitation attempts in the wild, and Canada's Cyber Centre updated its advisory to say open-source reporting indicated active exploitation, even as SonicWall initially said it had seen no confirmed evidence of a breach. Remote-access gateways sit at the edge of a company's network, so a maximum-severity, no-credentials-needed flaw exploited within days of disclosure is exactly the kind of incident that pushes IT and security budgets toward faster patching and closer vendor risk review.

Sales angle
A prospect running SonicWall SMA appliances — or any remote-access/VPN gateway — may be more receptive right now to a conversation about how quickly their vendors ship and communicate security patches.
SDR takeaway
"SSRF" tricks a server into making requests it shouldn't, often reaching internal systems; "pre-authentication" means no valid login is needed to exploit it, which is why this flaw is rated so severely.

Intuit to Acquire AI-Native Revenue Management Startup Sequence, Taking Aim at NetSuite's Mid-Market Turf

Intuit announced on October 15 that it has agreed to acquire Sequence, a London- and New York-based startup whose AI-native platform automates the full quote-to-revenue process — contract configuration, pricing, billing, invoicing, collections, and revenue recognition. Terms weren't disclosed, though several outlets cited media estimates near $200 million. Intuit plans to fold Sequence into Intuit Enterprise Suite and QuickBooks Online Advanced, explicitly targeting mid-market B2B software, professional-services, and other companies whose billing today runs alongside platforms including NetSuite. Sequence co-founder and CEO Riya Grover is set to join Intuit's mid-market product organization. Intuit is positioning the combined product as an alternative to legacy ERP systems for revenue and billing management — a direct push into the mid-market account base NetSuite has long treated as its home turf.

SAP Posts First-Ever €10 Billion Quarter as Cloud Backlog Hits €24.1 Billion

SAP's Q3 2026 results, released October 21, showed total revenue of €10.27 billion, up 13% as reported — the company's first quarter above €10 billion. Cloud revenue grew 26% as reported, and current cloud backlog reached €24.1 billion, up 28% from €18.8 billion a year earlier. Non-IFRS operating profit was €3.03 billion, up 18%, though cloud gross margin slipped slightly to 74.3% from 75.1%. SAP noted part of the quarter's growth reflected a multi-quarter revenue catch-up from customers returning from financial distress, and guided Q4 backlog growth to decelerate slightly from 2025's pace. SAP's cloud backlog — effectively contracted future revenue — is the number investors and competitors watch most closely, and its continued acceleration shows enterprise customers are still signing multi-year cloud ERP commitments even as some of this quarter's growth came from one-time catch-up effects.

Sales angle
A prospect currently on SAP, or comparing SAP against NetSuite, may be in the middle of a multi-year cloud commitment cycle — useful when evaluating how locked-in that prospect might already be.
SDR takeaway
"Backlog" (or "remaining performance obligation") is contracted revenue not yet recognized — a forward-looking number that often matters more to investors than the current quarter's reported revenue.
