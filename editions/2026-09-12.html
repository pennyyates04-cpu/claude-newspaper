Oracle Posts Record Q1 Results as AI Cloud Demand Accelerates

What happened: Oracle reported fiscal Q1 2027 results on September 10: total revenue of $19.3 billion, up 30% year-over-year, with cloud revenue (SaaS + IaaS) up 62% to $11.6 billion and cloud infrastructure (OCI) revenue up 121% to $7.4 billion — the ninth straight quarter of accelerating infrastructure growth. Remaining performance obligations (RPO) — Oracle's contracted-but-not-yet-billed backlog — hit $664 billion, up $209 billion year-over-year, and the company delivered 850 additional megawatts of AI data-center capacity in the quarter. Oracle raised its full-year guidance to at least $90 billion in revenue (34% growth) and $8.10 non-GAAP EPS. Why it matters: Oracle is NetSuite's parent company, and this quarter shows its AI infrastructure bet is converting into real bookings at massive scale, even as heavy capital spending keeps pressuring cash flow. It's useful context for any prospect conversation that touches Oracle's broader financial health or AI strategy.

Uber Cuts 10% of Its Workforce in Its Biggest Layoffs Since the Pandemic

What happened: Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi told employees on September 2 that the company is cutting about 3,300 jobs — roughly 10% of its global workforce — in its largest reduction since COVID-era cuts in 2020. The plan shrinks the number of managers by 20%, cuts small redundant teams in half, and removes staff more than seven layers below the CEO, redirecting the savings toward Uber's core ridesharing/delivery business and its robotaxi push. Khosrowshahi also said only about 1% of employees will remain fully remote going forward. Why it matters: Uber is profitable and growing, so this isn't a distress cut — it's a bet that a flatter organization can move faster on its next big initiative, a pattern showing up at other large, healthy tech companies too.

Sales angle
Companies going through this kind of "delayering" often revisit their software stack and vendor contracts at the same time, as team structures and approval chains change — worth watching for in similarly sized accounts.
SDR takeaway
"Delayering" — stripping out layers of middle management to speed up decisions — is becoming a common efficiency move at large companies, independent of financial distress.

Small Business Optimism Slips in August as Sales Weaken and Inflation Lingers

What happened: NFIB's Small Business Optimism Index fell 1.1 points to 98.7 in August (still above the 52-year average of 98.0), pulling back from July's one-year high of 99.8. Owners reported weaker sales over the prior three months, elevated uncertainty, and continued concern about inflation, even as complaints about labor costs and labor quality eased to multi-year lows. A net 17% of firms plan to add jobs over the next three months, down three points from July. Why it matters: This is a direct read on the health of the small- and mid-sized businesses that make up a large share of NetSuite's prospect base — softening sales and lingering uncertainty shape how cautious those buyers are about new software spending.

New Research: Cold Calls Still Beat AI-Written Emails 6-to-1 for Reaching Prospects

What happened: A survey of 153 sales leaders and reps by ValueSelling Associates and Selling Power, released September 1, found sellers rate human cold calls far more effective than AI-personalized email for reaching new prospects — 47% versus 8%, a roughly 6-to-1 gap, with only client referrals (74%) rated higher. Despite that, 39% of sellers said they still fear picking up the phone, and 46% admitted they give up on cold calling too easily. Why it matters: It's a timely counterpoint to the AI-SDR and AI-email hype cycle: for actually reaching a new prospect, live phone outreach still wins by a wide margin, even though the skill and confidence to do it well is what's often missing, not the tactic itself.

Sales angle
If a prospect's team is investing heavily in AI-outreach tools, this is a useful, non-confrontational data point that a blended approach — AI research plus real phone conversations — still outperforms an all-automated one.
SDR takeaway
Don't let AI tools crowd out the phone entirely; the data says cold calling remains the second-most effective way to reach a new prospect, right behind referrals.
