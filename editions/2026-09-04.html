Cisco Gives All 90,000 Employees a Personal AI Agent

Cisco has rolled out MyAgent, a personalized AI agent for its entire 90,000-person workforce, built on its internal Circuit platform. Each employee's agent draws on a shared pool of more than 800 subagents, and Cisco routes roughly half of all requests to lower-cost open-weight models to control spend, reserving frontier models for harder tasks. This is one of the largest enterprise-wide agentic AI deployments disclosed to date, and Cisco's cost-routing approach is a real-world blueprint for controlling AI spend at scale.

Sales angle
Prospects sizing their own AI rollout will ask "how did a company this size control costs?" — Cisco's mixed-model routing is a useful proof point in CFO or CIO conversations.
SDR takeaway

McKinsey: Hybrid AI-Plus-Human Sales Teams Generate 41% More Pipeline

New McKinsey research on B2B sales finds that companies deploying AI sales agents alongside human SDRs — rather than replacing reps with AI or ignoring AI altogether — generate up to 41% more sales pipeline than either approach alone. This gives a data-backed answer to "will AI replace SDRs" anxiety: the winning model pairs AI for volume (prospecting, initial outreach) with humans for judgment, relationship-building, and complex deals.
