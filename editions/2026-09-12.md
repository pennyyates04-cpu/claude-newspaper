## Oracle Posts Record Q1 Results as AI Cloud Demand Accelerates

## Uber Cuts 10% of Its Workforce in Its Biggest Layoffs Since the Pandemic

**What happened:** Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi told employees on September 2 that the company is cutting about 3,300 jobs — roughly 10% of its global workforce — in its largest reduction since COVID-era cuts in 2020. The plan shrinks the number of managers by 20%, cuts small redundant teams in half, and removes staff more than seven layers below the CEO, redirecting the savings toward Uber's core ridesharing/delivery business and its robotaxi push. Khosrowshahi also said only about 1% of employees will remain fully remote going forward.
**Why it matters:** Uber is profitable and growing, so this isn't a distress cut — it's a bet that a flatter organization can move faster on its next big initiative, a pattern showing up at other large, healthy tech companies too.

**Sales angle:** Companies going through this kind of "delayering" often revisit their software stack and vendor contracts at the same time, as team structures and approval chains change — worth watching for in similarly sized accounts.
**SDR takeaway:** "Delayering" — stripping out layers of middle management to speed up decisions — is becoming a common efficiency move at large companies, independent of financial distress.
**Conversation starter:** "We've seen a few larger companies flatten their management structure recently to move faster — has anything like that come up on your end?"

## New Research: Cold Calls Still Beat AI-Written Emails 6-to-1 for Reaching Prospects

**What happened:** A survey of 153 sales leaders and reps by ValueSelling Associates and Selling Power, released September 1, found sellers rate human cold calls far more effective than AI-personalized email for reaching new prospects — 47% versus 8%, a roughly 6-to-1 gap, with only client referrals (74%) rated higher. Despite that, 39% of sellers said they still fear picking up the phone, and 46% admitted they give up on cold calling too easily.
**Why it matters:** It's a timely counterpoint to the AI-SDR and AI-email hype cycle: for actually reaching a new prospect, live phone outreach still wins by a wide margin, even though the skill and confidence to do it well is what's often missing, not the tactic itself.

**Sales angle:** If a prospect's team is investing heavily in AI-outreach tools, this is a useful, non-confrontational data point that a blended approach — AI research plus real phone conversations — still outperforms an all-automated one.
**SDR takeaway:** Don't let AI tools crowd out the phone entirely; the data says cold calling remains the second-most effective way to reach a new prospect, right behind referrals.
**Conversation starter:** "New research says cold calls still outreach AI emails 6-to-1 for actually getting a response — has your team found the same thing, or leaned more into automated outreach?"
