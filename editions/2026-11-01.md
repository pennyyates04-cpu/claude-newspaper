## Critical SonicWall SMA1000 Flaw (CVSS 10.0) Exploited Within Days of Disclosure

**What happened:** SonicWall patched CVE-2026-102255 on October 6 — a pre-authentication server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability in the WorkPlace interface of its SMA1000 remote-access appliances (the 6210, 7210, and 8200v models), rated a maximum CVSS score of 10.0. Within days, researchers and outlets including The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, and SecurityWeek reported exploitation attempts in the wild, and Canada's Cyber Centre updated its advisory to say open-source reporting indicated active exploitation, even as SonicWall initially said it had seen no confirmed evidence of a breach.
**Why it matters:** Remote-access gateways sit at the edge of a company's network, so a maximum-severity, no-credentials-needed flaw exploited within days of disclosure is exactly the kind of incident that pushes IT and security budgets toward faster patching and closer vendor risk review.

**Sales angle:** A prospect running SonicWall SMA appliances — or any remote-access/VPN gateway — may be more receptive right now to a conversation about how quickly their vendors ship and communicate security patches, since that speed has become a real differentiator.
**SDR takeaway:** "SSRF" (server-side request forgery) tricks a server into making requests it shouldn't, often reaching internal systems; "pre-authentication" means no valid login is needed to exploit it, which is why this flaw is rated so severely.
**Conversation starter:** "There's been another maximum-severity vulnerability exploited within days of disclosure this month — how does your team typically prioritize patching for internet-facing systems?"

## SAP Posts First-Ever €10 Billion Quarter as Cloud Backlog Hits €24.1 Billion

**What happened:** SAP's Q3 2026 results, released October 21, showed total revenue of €10.27 billion, up 13% as reported — the company's first quarter above €10 billion. Cloud revenue grew 26% as reported, and current cloud backlog reached €24.1 billion, up 28% from €18.8 billion a year earlier. Non-IFRS operating profit was €3.03 billion, up 18%, though cloud gross margin slipped slightly to 74.3% from 75.1%. **Why it matters:** SAP's cloud backlog — effectively contracted future revenue — is the number investors and competitors watch most closely, and its continued acceleration shows enterprise customers are still signing multi-year cloud ERP commitments even as some of this quarter's growth came from one-time catch-up effects.

**SDR takeaway:** "Backlog" (or "remaining performance obligation") is contracted revenue not yet recognized — a forward-looking number that often matters more to investors than the current quarter's reported revenue.
