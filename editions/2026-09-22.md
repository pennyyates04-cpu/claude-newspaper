## Oracle Weaves Ask Oracle and a New CPQ AI Assistant Into Core NetSuite

**What happened:** With the NetSuite 2026.2 release, now rolling out in phases through the third quarter, Oracle brought its "Ask Oracle" conversational AI assistant — previously limited to the NetSuite Next preview — into the core NetSuite product for U.S. and Canada accounts first. Ask Oracle lets users type plain-language questions to search records, generate reports, and complete tasks instead of building a saved search. ## New G2 Research: Nearly Half of B2B Software Buyers Had a CFO Veto an Approved Purchase

**What happened:** G2's 2026 Buyer Behavior Report, based on a survey of more than 1,000 B2B software buyers and interviews with over 50 sales and marketing leaders, found that 49% of buyers said a CFO vetoed an already-approved software purchase in the last 12 months — rising to 54% at organizations with a dedicated AI/LLM budget. Finance involvement in software buying jumped from 31% to 46% in a year. Partly as a result, 7 in 10 buyers say AI's pace is pushing them toward shorter contracts, and buyers who've faced a late-stage veto push for sub-12-month terms at more than double the rate of those who haven't (40% vs. 18%).
**Why it matters:** It's hard evidence that finance teams, not just end users, have become active gatekeepers late in B2B software deals — a dynamic that can stall or kill deals that looked closed, and one that's pushing buyers toward shorter, lower-commitment contracts.

**Sales angle:** Directly relevant to closing NetSuite and other enterprise deals — getting finance (CFO/VP Finance) engaged early, rather than only at final approval, may reduce the risk of a late-stage veto derailing a deal that otherwise looked done.
**SDR takeaway:** When qualifying a deal, ask who on the finance side needs to sign off — a "yes" from a champion or department head is increasingly not the same as final approval.
**Conversation starter:** "I saw new research showing nearly half of software buyers had a CFO veto an already-approved purchase this year — has finance become a bigger part of your team's software approval process lately?"
