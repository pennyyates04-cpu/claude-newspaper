## New G2 Research: B2B Buyers Increasingly Start With AI Chatbots, But Few Trust AI to Close the Deal

**What happened:** G2's 2026 "Evaluation Maze" buyer behavior report, published July 22 and based on a survey of more than 1,000 B2B software buyers, found that 82% sourced software recommendations from an AI chatbot in the past two years. **Why it matters:** This is a direct, data-backed look at the gap between "AI is reshaping how buyers research" and "AI isn't replacing human judgment on actual purchase decisions" — worth knowing given AI sales hype in both directions.

**SDR takeaway:** "Evaluation stage" is the part of a buying process where a prospect compares vendors in depth (demos, trials, references) before deciding — G2's data says this stage is getting longer, not shorter, despite more AI-assisted research upfront.
