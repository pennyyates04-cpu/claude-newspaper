## Cisco Gives All 90,000 Employees a Personal AI Agent

**What happened:** Cisco has rolled out MyAgent, a personalized AI agent for its entire 90,000-person workforce, built on its internal Circuit platform. Each employee's agent draws on a shared pool of more than 800 subagents, and Cisco routes roughly half of all requests to lower-cost open-weight models to control spend, reserving frontier models for harder tasks.
**Why it matters:** This is one of the largest enterprise-wide agentic AI deployments disclosed to date, and Cisco's cost-routing approach is a real-world blueprint for controlling AI spend at scale.

**Sales angle:** Prospects sizing their own AI rollout will ask "how did a company this size control costs?" — Cisco's mixed-model routing is a useful proof point in CFO or CIO conversations.
